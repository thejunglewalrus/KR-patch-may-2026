Korean Lost Ark · Patch Translation

May 27 (Wed) Regular Maintenance Complete

Source: lostark.game.onstove.com — Notice #13449 · Posted 2026-05-27 09:59 KST

Direct translation. No commentary or extrapolation added.

Notice

Hello, this is Lost Ark. The May 27 regular maintenance has ended (10:00). Please refer to the following items applied during the regular maintenance.

Maintenance Contents

Combat / Just Guard

  • Adjusted the movement distance during Just Guard for some classes.

UI / Tooltips

  • Fixed so that when hovering over a gem that only has the ‘Support Effect +N%’ effect, the tooltip comparing it against the currently equipped gem is also displayed.

Combat Analyzer

  • Fixed an issue where DoT damage from Burn / Bleed / Poison / Electric, applied by skill Tripods, was not added to the skill damage values in the Combat Analyzer’s ‘Attack Info’ tab.

Summoner

  • Fixed an issue where, when Summoner summoned Pauru, the slot skill intermittently did not switch to its Chain Command skill in certain situations.

Kazeros Raid — Act 2: Requiem of the Floating Nightmare (Extreme)

  • Fixed an issue where, during the pattern in which ‘Avrelshud, Manifestor of Dreams’ performs a large screen-cleaving attack, the ‘Nightmare Pentanicle’ was, at a very low probability, not generated.

Kazeros Raid — Final Act: The Last Day (Gate 2)

  • Fixed an issue where the character performing a Collision was forcibly moved to the Collision progression position, but at a very low probability the Collision did not proceed and the character remained movable.

Machinist

  • Fixed an issue where, when using the Super Awakening ‘Project Titan’, the character’s face was intermittently rendered abnormally in certain situations.

Valkyrie

  • Eased an issue where the skill sound was played too quietly when using some Valkyrie skills that generate projectiles.
    • To ease this issue, the overall volume of Valkyrie skill sounds has been adjusted upward.

Coin of Courage — End of Service

  • The ‘Coin of Courage’ has been removed as its usage period has expired.
    • With the removal of Coin of Courage, the ‘Crest’ tab has been removed from the Item Codex’s ‘Special Equipment’ category.
    • With the removal of Coin of Courage, the ‘Proving Grounds Shop’ NPC placed in major cities has been removed.
    • With the removal of Coin of Courage, the ‘Hero’s Crest’ can no longer be exchanged or crafted.

Closing

We hope you enjoy a great day with Lost Ark today, and we will continue doing our best so that there are no inconveniences in your service experience. Thank you, as always.

  • 서머너 → Summoner; 파우루 → Pauru (Summoner’s elemental).

  • 화상 / 출혈 / 중독 / 전격 → Burn / Bleed / Poison / Electric — the four standard Tripod DoT damage types.
  • 카제로스 레이드 → Kazeros Raid; 2막: 부유하는 악몽의 진혼곡 → Act 2: Requiem of the Floating Nightmare; 종막: 최후의 날 → Final Act: The Last Day.
